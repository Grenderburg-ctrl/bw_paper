## 6. Permutation test (Fig. 4B)

No original permutation script (Python, seed 1) was found in the repository, so the test was re-implemented in R (`R/fig4.R`, `set.seed(1)`): for each of the 62 panel genes with padj, one gene is drawn uniformly from the 200 non-panel genes with padj nearest in log10(baseMean); 2000 permutations. The null distribution is in `source_data/Fig4B_null.csv`. Because the RNG differs from the Python original, exact agreement is not expected; agreement within Monte-Carlo error is.

| statistic | expected | obtained | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| down | 7.7 ± 2.5 | 7.6 ± 2.5 | ❌ |
| up | 9.2 ± 2.7 | 9.2 ± 2.7 | ✅ |
| down − up | −1.5 ± 4.1 | −1.6 ± 4.0 | ❌ |

Unrounded: down 7.60 ± 2.50 (MC s.e. of mean 0.06); up 9.19 ± 2.73 (MC s.e. of mean 0.06); down − up −1.59 ± 3.97 (MC s.e. of mean 0.09). The 0.1 differences from the expected values are about two Monte-Carlo standard errors of the mean, i.e. consistent with a different random stream (R vs. Python); the SDs agree.
