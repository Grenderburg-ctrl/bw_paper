# FigVG — draft legend (English)

**Figure X. Differential expression of voltage-gated Na⁺, Ca²⁺ and K⁺ channel genes and their auxiliary subunits in rLLB hearts relative to Control.** Forest plot of log₂ fold change (rLLB vs. Control; DESeq2, Wald test, n = 4 per group, no log₂FC shrinkage). Dots show log₂ fold change and whiskers show ± lfcSE (standard error of the log₂ fold change). (A) Nav and Cav; (B) Kv. Genes are grouped by family (pore-forming α subunits first, then auxiliary subunits). Blue, significantly decreased (padj < 0.05); red, significantly increased (padj < 0.05); grey, not significant (ns); no log₂ fold-change threshold was applied. padj, Benjamini–Hochberg-adjusted p value; * padj < 0.05, ** < 0.01, *** < 0.001, **** < 0.0001. Hollow markers, genes with low expression (mean DESeq2-normalised counts < 5 in at least one group; in Control ≈ 0 for the genes shown beyond the axis). Triangles at the edge of the axis, values beyond the plotted range (the log₂ fold change is given next to the triangle). Genes with padj = NA after independent filtering (not tested) and genes not detected (filtered at ≥ 10 counts over 8 samples, or absent) are listed below each panel and are not drawn. The list comprises 94 genes (60 core pore-forming and 34 auxiliary); 59 are shown. Gene families and protein names follow the IUPHAR/BPS Guide to Pharmacology nomenclature. Gene symbols are rat symbols.

Core: 10 down, 1 up, 24 ns, 4 not tested, 21 not detected. Auxiliary: 5 down, 1 up, 18 ns, 2 not tested, 8 not detected.
