**Figure 7. Western blot densitometry of PIEZO1 and TRPM4 in Control and rLLB hearts, and comparison with mRNA changes.**
(A, B) Band densitometry for PIEZO1 (A) and TRPM4 (B) (40 µg protein per lane). Primary antibodies: PIEZO1, ABclonal A23380 (1:1000); TRPM4, ABclonal A10146 (1:1000); secondary antibody: HRP-conjugated goat anti-rabbit IgG, ABclonal AS014 (1:10,000). Integrated band density (ImageJ) was normalised to the mean of the Control group of the same blot, set to 1; densitometry was normalised to the Control mean only, and normalisation to a loading control or total protein stain was not performed. Bars show mean ± SEM; dots are individual animals (n = 4 animals per group; the order of dots within a group carries no pairing information). P values: Welch t-test (two-sided, unpaired) on the normalised densitometry, exact values shown. PIEZO1 in rLLB was 0.57 of Control (p = 0.013); TRPM4 was unchanged (1.05 of Control, p = 0.89).
(C) mRNA versus protein. Circles, mRNA (RNA-seq, DESeq2): log₂ fold change (rLLB vs. Control) ± lfcSE for *Piezo1* and *Trpm4*, with Benjamini–Hochberg-adjusted p values (padj). Squares, protein (Western blot): difference of the mean log₂ normalised densities (rLLB − Control) ± Welch standard error, with the Welch p value. Blue, significant decrease (padj or p < 0.05); grey, not significant. The dashed line marks no change.
The lower PIEZO1 protein level is consistent with the decreased *Piezo1* mRNA, whereas TRPM4 protein did not change despite decreased *Trpm4* mRNA. With n = 4 per group and normalisation to the Control mean only, these data are consistent with, but do not confirm, the mRNA results.
