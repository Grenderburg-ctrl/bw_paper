**Figure 7 (total protein). Western blot densitometry of PIEZO1 and TRPM4 in Control and rLLB hearts, normalised to Ponceau, and comparison with mRNA changes.**
(A, B) Band densitometry for PIEZO1 (A) and TRPM4 (B) (40 µg protein per lane). Primary antibodies: PIEZO1, ABclonal A23380 (1:1000); TRPM4, ABclonal A10146 (1:1000); secondary antibody: HRP-conjugated goat anti-rabbit IgG, ABclonal AS014 (1:10,000). Integrated band density (ImageJ) was divided by the Ponceau total-protein signal of the same lane, then by the mean of the Control lane ratios of the same blot, set to 1. Bars show mean ± SEM; dots are individual animals (n = 4 animals per group; the order of dots within a group carries no pairing information). P values: Welch t-test (two-sided, unpaired) on the total-protein-normalised densitometry, exact values shown. With the provisional Ponceau loads, PIEZO1 in rLLB was 0.59 of Control (p = 0.027); TRPM4 was unchanged (1.07 of Control, p = 0.85).
(C) mRNA versus protein. Circles, mRNA (RNA-seq, DESeq2): log₂ fold change (rLLB vs. Control) ± lfcSE for *Piezo1* and *Trpm4*, with Benjamini–Hochberg-adjusted p values (padj). Squares, protein (Western blot): difference of the mean log₂ normalised densities (rLLB − Control) ± Welch standard error, with the Welch p value. Blue, significant decrease (padj or p < 0.05); grey, not significant. The dashed line marks no change.
The lower PIEZO1 protein level remains in the same direction as the decreased *Piezo1* mRNA. TRPM4 protein did not change. The Ponceau values in `data/ponceau_piezo1.csv` and `data/ponceau_trpm4.csv` are provisional relative loads from one photographed membrane (15% of the measured lane-to-lane deviation); replace them with ImageJ integrated density of each blot before using this panel in the manuscript. The control-mean-only figure is unchanged (`figures/Fig7.pdf`).
